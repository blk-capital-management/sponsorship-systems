**Attendees:** Fola (BLK Co-Sponsorship Chair, US), Daniel (BLK EMEA Co-Head), plus two Sixth Street representatives (one new since August, filling in for Maddie/Matty on partnerships)

### **Purpose**

Annual membership audit with Sixth Street to gather feedback on the partnership and discuss the US and EMEA sides. Sixth Street confirmed they are currently on the **Gold tier through September**.

### **Sixth Street's Feedback (US Side)**

The most valuable part of the partnership for them is BLK acting as a channel to spread awareness of Sixth Street's programs, spotlight events, and internship postings. As a smaller firm relative to the large banks, they are focused on building name recognition, especially among junior students exploring finance. They were pleased with the number of BLK participants in their programs this past year. (Rep noted limited perspective, having only joined in August.)

### **Gold Tier Benefits Reiterated**

Access to Fall and Spring conferences (Fall is the in-person flagship), spotlight and insight calls for promoting job postings, recruiter access, firm-match resume books (tailored candidates), and networking/recruiting dinners.

### **Recruitment Process Clarified**

* **Timeline:** Next cycle is the 2027 summer internship program, typically opening around September (possibly earlier). Sixth Street will keep BLK in the loop.  
* **Resume books:** Led by Sixth Street — they email BLK when they need candidates; BLK turns it around within \~a week. Books are drawn from \~350 verified active members (audit responses), curated specifically for members who indicated interest in the firm/industry and tailored to specific job postings.  
* **Job postings:** Sixth Street sends postings to BLK, which distributes broadly across newsletters, Slack, and all members; resume books handle the niche/targeted talent.

### **BLK Overview Provided**

BLK was described as a student-run nonprofit and investment fund focused on removing barriers for underrepresented students in finance — mentorship, structured analyst training (pathway programs across alternative investments, IB, equity research), spotlight/insight calls, members ranging freshman to 4th year, spanning IB, PE, alternative investments, and private credit.
